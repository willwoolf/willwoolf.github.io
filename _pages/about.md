I am a postgraduate researcher in the [School of Computer Science](https://eps.leeds.ac.uk/computing) at the University of Leeds.
Currently, I am researching dynamic floating-point error analysis for scientific computing applications.

Before coming to Leeds, I attended the University of Manchester where I received an MMath in Mathematics (2020-2024).
My background is in scientific computing and numerical linear algebra.

At the moment, my work involves programming in Julia.
I am also familiar with C++, Python and MATLAB.
